Manamum Gunamum

Manamum Gunamum is a small, private automation tool used by the owner of the Manamum Gunamum YouTube channel.

What this app does

The channel owner records short self-development videos himself. This tool uploads those finished video files to his own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API, together with the titles and descriptions he has written for them. That is the tool's only function.

Who uses it

The tool is used by exactly one person: the owner of the Manamum Gunamum channel, on his own Google account and his own channel. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

Google account access

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth sign-in), the tool receives access to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. It does not read, collect, or store any other person's data. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
